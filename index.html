Our Story

Southern flavours,
Goan spirit

Dosa & Podi brings the authentic tastes of South India to Goa. Every dish — from our crisp benne dosas to soft tatte idlis — follows traditional recipes made with the freshest ingredients.

Our name comes from two things we love: the perfect dosa — golden, buttery, crisp — and podi, the aromatic gunpowder spice blend that makes everything better. Whether you're stopping for a quick breakfast or a leisurely banana leaf meal, you're welcome here.
